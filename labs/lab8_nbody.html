📘 Theory

The N-body problem studies the collective gravitational motion of many interacting particles.

  • Direct summation: O(N²)
  • Tree methods (Barnes–Hut): O(N log N)
  • Used in star clusters, galaxies, cosmology
  • Large N reveals emergent structure
  • Relaxation, mixing, and core collapse
  • Numerical methods dominate feasibility
