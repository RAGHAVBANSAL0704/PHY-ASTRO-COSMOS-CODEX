Compare half-life curves, activity, and stochastic fluctuations.

📘 Theory

Radioactive decay follows an exponential law:

N(t) = N₀ e^{-λt}, with half-life T½ = ln(2)/λ

Activity is defined as A(t) = λN(t).

  • Stochastic decay shows fluctuations about the mean
  • Shorter half-life → faster activity drop
  • Log plots reveal linear trends
