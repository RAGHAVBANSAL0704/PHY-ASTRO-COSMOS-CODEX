ISS Orbit and Ground Track Simulator

Follow a satellite orbit and its moving footprint on Earth.

📘 Theory

The International Space Station (ISS) orbits Earth in Low Earth Orbit (LEO).

This simulator uses a Kepler-only approximation to visualize an orbit from TLE elements (not full SGP4).

  • Earth-centered inertial (ECI) frame
  • Keplerian elements
  • Perturbations (J₂, drag – optional)

Key Insight:

  • TLEs encode orbital elements, not positions
  • SGP4 propagator converts elements → position
  • Ground track shows Earth’s rotation effect
