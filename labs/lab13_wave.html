1D Wave Equation Simulator

Animate a vibrating string and track wave energy over time.

📘 Theory

The 1D wave equation is:

∂²u/∂t² = c² ∂²u/∂x²

The explicit finite-difference scheme is stable when c Δt/Δx ≤ 1.
