Diffusion Random Walk Simulator

Build a probability distribution from many tiny random steps.

📘 Theory

A 1D random walk gives ⟨x²⟩ = 2 D t, where D is the diffusion coefficient.

For step size ℓ and time step Δt, the diffusion coefficient is D = ℓ²/(2Δt).
