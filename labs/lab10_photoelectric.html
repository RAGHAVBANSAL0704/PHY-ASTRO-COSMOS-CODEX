📘 Theory

Einstein’s photoelectric equation:

Kmax = h f − φ

The stopping potential is Vs = Kmax/e (in volts when K is in eV).

  • Threshold frequency f₀ = φ/h
  • Intensity controls current, not Kmax
  • Stopping potential increases linearly with f
