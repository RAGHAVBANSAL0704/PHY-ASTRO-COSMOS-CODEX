Gravitational Lensing Simulator

Bend light with mass and watch rings, arcs, and duplicated images appear.

Core Idea

Mass curves spacetime, so light from a background source can appear shifted, stretched, or multiplied around a foreground lens.

When source, lens, and observer align, the image can become an Einstein ring.
