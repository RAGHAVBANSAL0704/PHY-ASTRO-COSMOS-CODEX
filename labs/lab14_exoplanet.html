Exoplanet Transit Simulator

Measure tiny dips in starlight to infer planet size, orbit, and alignment.

Core Idea

When a planet crosses in front of its star, it blocks a small fraction of light. The dip depth is approximately (planet radius / star radius)².

  • Larger planets make deeper dips.
  • Shorter orbital periods repeat more quickly.
  • High inclination can miss the star completely.
