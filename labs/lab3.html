📘 Theory

Newton's Law of Gravitation:

F = -GMm / r²

Equations of Motion (relative coordinate):

d²r/dt² = -GM r / r³

Conserved Quantities:

  • Total Energy: E = ½v² -GM/r
  • Angular Momentum: L = r * v

Key Insight:

  • Bound orbits occur when E < 0
  • Angular momentum conservation fixes orbital plane
  • Symplectic integrators preserve orbits long-term
