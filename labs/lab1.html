📘 Theory

    
Equations of Motion (1D):

    
dx/dt = v

    
dv/dt = a (constant)


    
Analytical Solution:

    
v(t) = v₀ + at

    
x(t) = x₀ + v₀t + ½at²


    

      In this experiment, we compare the exact analytical solution with the numerical
      solution obtained using the Euler forward method.
