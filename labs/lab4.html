📘 Theory

The three-body problem follows Newton’s laws exactly, yet exhibits chaotic motion.

Tiny perturbations in initial conditions cause trajectories to diverge exponentially.

  • The system is deterministic but not predictable
  • No general analytical solution exists
  • Lyapunov growth characterizes chaos
