📘 Theory

Kepler’s First Law: Planetary orbits are ellipses with the Sun at one focus.

Kepler’s Second Law: Equal areas are swept in equal times.

Kepler’s Third Law: T² ∝ a³

  • All three laws follow from Newtonian gravity
  • Kepler 2nd law ⇔ angular momentum conservation
