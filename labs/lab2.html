📘 Theory

System: 1D Harmonic Oscillator

Force law: F = −kx

Equations:

dx/dt = v

dv/dt = −(k/m)x

Total Energy:

E = ½mv² + ½kx² (should be conserved)

Key Insight:

  • Euler is unstable for oscillatory systems
  • Verlet is symplectic → good energy conservation
  • RK4 is accurate short-term but not symplectic
